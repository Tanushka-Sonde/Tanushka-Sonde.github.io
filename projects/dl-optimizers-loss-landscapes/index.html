Deep Learning Optimizers, Loss Landscapes & Regularization

Optimizers on classic loss surfaces, Adam learning-rate schedules, and dropout on FashionMNIST.

Aug 2025 · Group Project, IIT Gandhinagar, Deep Learning, Prof. Nipun Batra

GitHub

What I did

  • Analyzed GD, Momentum, SGD, Adam, and Mini-Batch SGD on bowl, ravine, saddle, and Rosenbrock loss surfaces, visualizing convergence through animated trajectory GIFs.
  • Benchmarked six Adam learning-rate schedules — constant, step decay, exponential, cosine annealing, and warmup-cosine — identifying cosine annealing as the most robust choice.
  • Implemented dropout regularization on FashionMNIST, sweeping probabilities from 0.0 to 0.75, and showed p=0.5 minimized the train-test generalization gap at 87.3% accuracy.
  • Formulated a differentiable lamp-placement optimization problem using Gaussian brightness fields, maximizing room illumination via gradient-based Adam optimization.

Tech: GD, Momentum, SGD, Adam, FashionMNIST
