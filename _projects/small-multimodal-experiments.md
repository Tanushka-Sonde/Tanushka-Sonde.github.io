## What I did

- Implemented a **symmetric CLIP contrastive loss** from scratch, scoring 0.0002 aligned versus 9.615 permuted.
- Built a **semantic image retrieval engine** over images using frozen Embedding and cosine ranking.
- Fine-tuned only SmolVLM's **7,077,888-parameter connector**, lifting digit accuracy from 76 to 98 percent in 100 steps.
- Built a **concept bottleneck intervention pipeline**, flipping a prediction via one corrected concept score.

**Tech:** CLIP, SmolVLM
